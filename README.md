# José Humberto Torres-Villanueva

**Doctoral Researcher | Mixed Methods | Mental Health Research | Responsible AI | Data & Research Technologies**

Doctoral researcher at **El Colegio de la Frontera Sur (ECOSUR), Mexico**, currently enrolled in the doctoral program in **Ecology and Sustainable Development**.

I work at the intersection of **social and mental health research, mixed methods, data analysis, responsible artificial intelligence, and research technologies**. My academic background combines social research with information and communication technologies, allowing me to approach research questions from both methodological and computational perspectives.

My broader interest is in research where **human wellbeing, social systems, data, ethics, and technology intersect**.

## Why most of my repositories are private

A substantial part of my development activity is maintained in **private repositories** because it involves production systems, organizational infrastructure, confidential research, privacy-sensitive information, or projects subject to ethical and data-protection requirements.

For that reason, much of this work remains private by design.

I prioritize:

* Participant confidentiality
* Research ethics
* Responsible data governance
* Organizational confidentiality
* Secure development practices
* Reproducibility without exposing protected information

Public repositories on this profile are intended to share **non-sensitive research tools, reproducible examples, methodological experiments, synthetic-data demonstrations, and open technical work**.
